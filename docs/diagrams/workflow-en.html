The workflow in words

  1. Brief: Capture the brand name, exact lettering, industry, audience, style, and intended use. Ask about missing details or record them as assumptions.
  2. Create and refine: The Codex native image tool generates separate concept images and edits the chosen image. Originals, revisions, the exact prompts, and parent image relationships are preserved in the local project.
  3. Review and deliver: Codex visually checks the lettering, composition, and readability at the intended size. The file helper verifies PNG format, visible pixels, background requirements, and hashes, then writes the selected PNG, ZIP, and brand guide to a new output folder.
